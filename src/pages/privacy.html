Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information Cash.Work ("we", "us") collects when you use cash.work, how we use it and the choices you have. We keep collection to what's needed to run the site, answer you and fund the service.

Information you give us

When you use a form on the site (the matching quiz, job alerts, contest entries, employer requests, applications, pledges or the contact form) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, country, phone number if you add one, and your message or answers. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service that processes the data on our behalf.

We use this information to respond to you, send the plan or alerts you asked for, run contests, review listings and applications, and, where you have agreed in the form, introduce you to employers or platforms that match your answers. We don't sell your personal information.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our host (GitHub Pages) records technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability. The calculators run entirely in your browser; numbers you enter into them are not sent to us.

Cookies and advertising

We use a small amount of browser storage to remember, for example, that you closed the cookie notice. We show ads through Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown.

Embedded content and links

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play; at that point YouTube's privacy policy applies. Donations are processed by PayPal under its own privacy policy; we receive the donor name, email and amount from PayPal. Job listings and news link to third-party websites whose practices we don't control.

How long we keep information

We keep form messages for as long as needed to handle your request and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires longer retention. Contest entries are kept for 12 months after the contest closes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of California can request disclosure of the categories of information collected and ask us not to sell or share it (we don't sell it). To make a request, use the contact form and choose "Privacy request". Every alert email includes an unsubscribe option.

Children

Cash.Work is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. Contests are open only to people aged 18 or older.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and our inbox address is not published in the page source. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, so please don't send sensitive data such as ID or bank numbers through our forms.

Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact form.
