Built for people who need to earn soon

Most lists of ways to make money are written for clicks. They mix real jobs with survey apps, skip the costs, and never tell you when you'll actually get paid. Cash.Work exists to fix that.

What we do

We keep a directory of legitimate platforms that pay people for work: delivery and rideshare apps, shift apps, home task marketplaces, pet and child care, freelance marketplaces, tutoring, paid research, AI training work, and ways to sell or rent out what you already own. For each one we record typical gross pay, how quickly earnings reach you, what you need to start and which countries it operates in.

Alongside the directory, the site pulls in remote jobs from public job boards, gig economy headlines and new videos from working creators every six hours, and offers free calculators that show what you really keep after costs and taxes.

How we research

  • We read each platform's own worker pages, help centres and pay explanations.
  • We compare pay figures across independent sources and worker reports, and publish ranges rather than single numbers.
  • We rate payout speed from each platform's stated payout options.
  • We re-check listings regularly and correct anything readers report.

How we make money

Cash.Work is funded by display advertising through Google AdSense, labelled sponsorships, reader donations and introductions between employers and people looking for work who have asked to be connected. Advertisers and sponsors never pay for a better rating or a higher position in the directory.

Independence

Cash.Work is an independent publication. It is not affiliated with any platform it lists, or with any other business that uses a similar name.
