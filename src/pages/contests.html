Official rules

  1. Sponsor. The contest is run by Cash.Work. It is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by any platform mentioned in entries.
  2. No purchase necessary. Entry is free. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances of winning.
  3. Eligibility. Open to individuals aged 18 or older where such contests are legal. Void where prohibited. Cash.Work staff and contractors may not enter.
  4. Entry period. Each monthly contest opens at 00:00 UTC on the first day of the month and closes at 23:59 UTC on the last day. One entry per person per month.
  5. Judging. Entries are judged on the criteria above by the Cash.Work editorial team. Entries must be original, true and written by the entrant.
  6. Prize. One winner per month receives $100 USD (or the equivalent in local currency) sent by PayPal or bank transfer, plus a feature on the site. The prize cannot be exchanged. If fewer than 10 eligible entries are received, Cash.Work may roll the contest into the next month.
  7. Skill-testing question. Winners resident in Canada must correctly answer a mathematical skill-testing question before the prize is awarded.
  8. Winner notification. The winner is contacted by email within seven days of the close. If they don't reply within 14 days, another entrant may be selected.
  9. Publicity. By entering, you allow Cash.Work to edit and publish your story with your first name and country. Your email is never published.
  10. Taxes. Winners are responsible for any taxes on prizes.
  11. Privacy. Entry details are handled under our privacy policy.
  12. Changes. Cash.Work may cancel or change the contest if it cannot run as planned, with notice on this page.
