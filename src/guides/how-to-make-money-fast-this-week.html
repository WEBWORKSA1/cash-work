How to make money this week without falling for scams

Needing money in a hurry is exactly when bad options look tempting. These are the legit routes that can put cash in your account within days, ranked by how quickly they usually pay.

By the Cash.Work editorial team. Updated November 19, 2026. 7 min read.

Fastest: sell something you already own

Local classifieds and marketplace apps turn unused things into cash the same day, with no approval process. Furniture, bikes, tools, game consoles, phones, baby gear and sports equipment sell quickly. Photograph items in daylight, price slightly below similar listings, and meet buyers in a public place. Take cash or a transfer that has cleared before you hand anything over.

Within a day or two: neighbourhood jobs

Offer help with moving, yard work, snow clearing, cleaning, furniture assembly or tech setup through community apps, local groups and word of mouth. Neighbours often pay on the day. Be specific in your post: what you'll do, when you're free and your price.

Within days: instant cash-out apps

Delivery, rideshare and grocery-shopping apps let you cash out earnings the same day once you're active. The catch is approval: background checks and document reviews take time, so apply to two or three on the same evening. Read how instant cash-out works before you start.

Within a week: shift apps and pet care

Shift apps fill warehouse, event, hospitality and retail shifts, often at short notice, and many pay within a few days of each shift. Pet-sitting platforms let you take dog walks and drop-ins near home and typically pay a couple of days after each booking. Both need a profile and some checks, so start today.

Not fast, but worth starting now

Freelance work, tutoring, paid research studies and AI training work can pay well but usually pay after a client or researcher approves your work. Start one of these alongside a fast option so next month is easier than this one.

What to avoid under pressure

  • Anything that asks you to pay first: starter kits, training fees, "deposits" to unlock tasks.
  • Cheques from strangers with a request to send part of the money back.
  • High-cost borrowing such as payday loans, which can turn a short gap into a long debt. If you're in a crisis, contact your bank, a non-profit credit counsellor or local assistance programmes first.
  • "Get rich quick" courses and gambling-style apps sold as income.

Your plan for the next seven days

  1. List two items for sale and post one neighbourhood-job offer today.
  2. Apply to two instant cash-out apps and one shift app tonight.
  3. Use the cash goal planner to set a daily target.
  4. Work your first shifts as approvals come through, and cash out once a day.
  5. At the end of the week, check your real hourly pay and keep what works.
