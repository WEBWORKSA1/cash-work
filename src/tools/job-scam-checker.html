Is this job offer a scam?

Tick every statement that's true about the offer. The more you tick, the higher the risk.

What to do

    If you think it's a scam

    1. Stop replying and don't send money, gift card codes, crypto or documents.
    2. If you've shared bank details, call your bank now and ask them to watch or freeze the account.
    3. Report it: in the US to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov, in Canada to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre, in the UK to Action Fraud, in India on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, and to the platform where you found the ad.
    4. Warn others. Tell us and we'll flag the pattern for readers.

    This checker is a guide, not a guarantee. A legit-looking offer can still be fake, so verify every employer independently. Read the full guide to avoiding work-from-home scams.
