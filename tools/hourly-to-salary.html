Hourly to salary, both ways

Compare a shift job with a salaried offer, or see what a gig rate adds up to over a year.

The quick rule

At 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, a year has 2,080 paid hours. So an hourly rate times 2,080 gives the annual figure, and a salary divided by 2,080 gives the hourly equivalent. A $22 hourly job is about $45,760 a year; a $55,000 salary is about $26.44 an hour.

Comparing gig work with a salary

A salary usually comes with paid holidays, employer pension or retirement contributions and, in many countries, employer-paid health coverage and payroll taxes. Gig and freelance pay doesn't. To compare fairly, reduce the paid weeks for gig work to reflect time off, and run the gig figure through the gig pay calculator first to remove costs.

Freelancers should set rates with the freelance rate calculator, which accounts for unbillable time and business costs.
