Paid research studies: earn for your opinions without the survey grind

Survey apps often pay a few dollars an hour. Paid research studies are different: companies and universities pay for focused time with the right people, and a single interview can pay more than a week of surveys.

By the Cash.Work editorial team. Updated November 19, 2026. 6 min read.

How research studies differ from surveys

Survey panels pay small amounts for short questionnaires and screen many people out. Research platforms connect you with a specific study: a 30 to 60 minute video interview about how you use a product, a usability test of a new app, a diary study over several days, or an academic experiment. Because researchers need particular kinds of participants, the pay per hour is usually much higher.

What they pay

User Interviews lists typical incentives of $20 to $300 or more for surveys and user tests, with online studies reaching higher for specialist participants and in-person sessions paying $40 to $1,000 or more. Prolific requires researchers to pay at least a minimum hourly rate. Professional studies, where companies want to hear from people in specific jobs, are often the best paid of all.

The trade-off is volume. Studies depend on what researchers need, so you might qualify for several in one month and none the next. Treat research as a high-value top-up, not a reliable main income.

Platforms to try

  • User Interviews: interviews, focus groups and product tests for companies; incentives are shown before you apply and are paid within ten business days of a completed session.
  • Prolific: academic and AI research studies with a guaranteed minimum hourly rate, paid through PayPal.
  • Respondent: higher-paying sessions, especially for professionals.
  • UserTesting: record yourself using websites and apps while you talk through your thoughts.

Compare them in the paid research section of the directory.

How to qualify more often

  1. Complete your profile fully and honestly. Matching depends on it. Job title, industry, devices, household and the products you use all open up studies.
  2. Answer screeners accurately. Inconsistent answers get accounts removed, and researchers can tell.
  3. Respond fast. Popular studies fill within hours. Turn on notifications.
  4. Be a great participant. Show up on time, test your microphone and camera, and give specific, thoughtful answers. Good ratings lead to more invitations.
  5. Join two or three platforms so you see more of the studies that fit you.

Stay safe

Legit research platforms never charge you to join and don't ask for bank details outside their own payment system. Be cautious with "studies" that arrive by text or chat app and ask you to install software or pay a fee. If in doubt, use the job scam checker.

Taxes

Research incentives are generally taxable income in most countries, even when paid as gift cards. Keep a simple record of what you receive. See tax basics for gig workers.

This guide is general information, not financial, tax or legal advice. Platforms change their pay and rules often; check the official site before you sign up.
