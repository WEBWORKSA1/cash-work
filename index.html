Questions people ask first

Which gig apps pay the same day?

Rideshare and delivery apps such as Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Grubhub and Instacart let you cash out the same day, usually for a small fee or free with their own debit card. Local cash jobs from neighbours and classifieds pay on the spot.

Is Cash.Work free?

Yes. Browsing, the calculators and the matching quiz are free. The site is supported by ads, sponsors and reader donations.

Do you hire workers or pay me directly?

No. Cash.Work is an independent guide. You apply and get paid through each platform or employer directly.

How often is the remote job list updated?

Every six hours, automatically, from public job feeds including Remotive, Jobicy, Himalayas, Arbeitnow and Remote OK.

How do I avoid work-from-home scams?

Never pay to apply, never cash a cheque and send money back, and verify the employer on its own website. The job scam checker walks you through the warning signs.
