Getting hired faster from a remote job board

Remote postings attract hundreds of applicants in the first 48 hours, so speed and fit matter more than polish. Apply within a day of a posting going live, mirror two or three phrases from the job description in your first lines, and link to one piece of proof, such as a portfolio, a GitHub profile or a short Loom video.

Check where the company can legally hire. Many "remote" roles are limited to one country or a few time zones. The location shown on each job above tells you who can apply.
