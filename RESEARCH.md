## Findings that shaped the build

1. Every competitor ranks by pay or popularity. None makes **payout speed** the primary sort. That is Cash.Work's angle and matches the domain's meaning.
2. Quizzes (Side Hustle Nation, Side Hustle School) are the strongest lead magnets in the niche. Cash.Work's matcher shows three results free and unlocks the rest plus a plan with an email.
3. Employers pay for reach: We Work Remotely charges for 30-day listings and Wellfound sells managed recruiting. Cash.Work offers free, featured and partner tiers.
4. Calculators (SmartAsset, Indeed Flex, Keeper) pull repeat traffic and links. Cash.Work ships five.
5. Public remote-job APIs (Remotive, Jobicy, Himalayas, Arbeitnow, Remote OK) allow a self-updating board on free static hosting, provided each listing is credited and linked back.
6. Trust is the niche's weak point (scams, inflated earnings). Clear disclosures, gross-vs-net framing and a scam checker differentiate and support AdSense approval.
